Color guide
  • Time of day. Daylight is cooler at noon and warmer at dawn and dusk, and indoor bulbs (about 2700 K warm to 5000 K cool) shift everything again. Judge colors in the light they will live in.
  • Your display. Screens differ in gamut (sRGB vs P3), brightness and calibration. An uncalibrated panel can be visibly off; only a calibrator or a physical proof tells you the truth.
  • Night mode. Night Shift, True Tone, f.lux and blue-light filters tint the whole screen. Turn them off when judging color or grading.
  • Print and paint. CMYK cannot reproduce every screen color, paper and sheen change the result, and paint looks different wet, dry and beside other colors. Always test a physical sample.
  • Contrast. WCAG asks for 4.5:1 on body text and 3:1 on large text and UI parts. 7:1 is AAA.
